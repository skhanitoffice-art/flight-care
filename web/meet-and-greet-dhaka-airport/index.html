Flight Care offers the most trusted and reliable Dhaka Airport Meet and Greet Service at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport (HSIA), Dhaka, Bangladesh. Whether you are arriving in Dhaka from abroad, catching an international departure, or connecting on a domestic flight, our dedicated protocol officers ensure a seamless, hassle-free airport journey.

What We Provide:

  • Aerobridge & Gate Reception: Warm welcome right at the aircraft exit or arrival concourse with a personalized name placard.
  • Fast-Track Guidance: Dedicated escort through immigration queues, document verification, and customs clearance.
  • Luggage Porter Assistance: Retrieval of baggage from luggage carousels, heavy trolley handling, and safe loading to vehicle.
  • Wheelchair & Special Passenger Care: Gentle assistance for elderly parents, sick patients, and first-time travelers.
  • Pay After Service: Book online with zero advance deposit; pay securely only after your service is fully delivered.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Where will the Flight Care protocol officer meet me?

On arrival, our officer will meet you immediately as you exit the aerobridge or enter the arrival terminal gate with a personalized placard showing your name.

Can I pay after service at Dhaka Airport?

Yes, Flight Care offers 100% peace of mind with 'Payment After Service'. You can pay via Cash, bKash, Nagad, or Bank Card after your service is completed.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 3 to 6 hours before flight arrival or departure, though emergency bookings are accommodated 24/7.

Zero advance deposit required. Pay securely after service completion at Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport.
